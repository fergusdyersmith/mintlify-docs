**Company objectives** are the cycle's headline goals — one line each, ambitious and qualitative. Everyone sees them (on the [Company page](/dashboard/company-page)), the CEO's exec OKRs ladder into them directly, and AI OKR suggestions use them as grounding.

## Creating one

Only owners can manage company objectives, and it happens inline on the **Company Dashboard**:

<Steps>
  <Step title="New company objective">
    In **Progress against company objectives**, click **New company objective**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the objective">
    One line, ambitious, qualitative — e.g. _"Become the default OKR tool for European scale-ups"_. Optionally add **Context** (_"What's driving it?"_).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save objective">
    It's visible company-wide immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing and deleting

Edit inline from the same panel. Deleting asks for confirmation (**"Delete this company objective?"**) and removes it permanently.

## How progress works

Company objectives don't have their own key results — the dashboard shows company-wide OKR progress against them, versus how much of the cycle has passed. The theory: if everyone's OKRs ladder into these objectives, aggregate progress _is_ company progress.

<Tip>
  Keep the list short — three to five. Ten "priorities" is another way of saying none.
</Tip>
