On **Admin → People**, switch the view toggle from **List** to **Org chart** to see the company as a tree of reporting lines.

## What it shows

- The hierarchy built from each person's **manager** — the same reporting lines that drive Team Dashboards and OKR-setting rights.
- **CEO** and **Owner** badges on the relevant people.
- **Expand all** to open every branch at once.

## "Not connected to the top of the chart"

People whose reporting line doesn't reach the top of the company are flagged. This usually means:

- They have **no manager** and aren't meant to be top-level — assign them a manager from the List view.
- Their manager chain contains a gap (e.g. their manager was removed from the company).

Disconnected people still work normally — they check in, hold OKRs — but they self-serve their own OKRs (no manager holds the pen) and appear on no one's Team Dashboard.

<Tip>
  After any restructure or removal, open the org chart once — it's the fastest way to spot orphaned reporting lines.
</Tip>
