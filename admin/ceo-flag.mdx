In **Admin → People**, one person per company can be marked **Chief / CEO**. As the setting explains: _"Exec OKRs are driven directly by the company objectives."_

## What the flag changes

- The CEO's **Personal Dashboard** shows a read-only **Company objectives** block above their own OKRs — the company's direction is always in view.
- Their own OKR section is relabelled **My exec OKRs**.
- They can always **add and edit their own OKRs**, even though they typically have no manager to do it for them.

## What it doesn't change

The flag grants **no admin permissions** — it's about how the dashboard frames their goals, not access. Make the CEO an **Owner** too if they should manage the company. See [Roles and permissions](/admin/roles-and-permissions).

## Setting it

In **Admin → People**, open the person's options and mark them as CEO. Only one person can hold the flag — assigning it moves it.

<Tip>
  The intended chain: [company objectives](/admin/company-objectives) → the CEO's exec OKRs → each team's OKRs via managers. The flag makes the top link visible on the CEO's own dashboard.
</Tip>
