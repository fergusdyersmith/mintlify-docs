The **Company Dashboard** is the owner's single screen for how the whole company is tracking this cycle.

## Current cycle strip

The cycle label (e.g. **Q3 2026**), **Week X of Y**, and **Pace** — average OKR progress versus how much of the cycle has elapsed, shown as ahead (\+) or behind (−) with a "today" marker on the progress bar.

## Mini-stats

- **Checked in** — how many people have submitted this week.
- **Open blockers** — blockers reported across all teams.
- **Time left** — weeks remaining in the cycle.

## Progress against company objectives

Each company objective with its progress — and this is also where you create, edit, and delete them. See [Company objectives](/admin/company-objectives).

## Per-team progress

A bar per team, aggregated from members' OKRs — how each part of the company is tracking.

## How the business is feeling

The company-wide mood mix from this cycle's check-ins.

## AI summary panels

**Feedback themes** (what people are talking about) and **Blocker themes** (what's getting in the way) — see [AI insights](/admin/ai-insights).

<Tip>
  A weekly owner routine: check **Pace**, then **Checked in**, then read the two AI theme panels — five minutes for a defensible picture of the whole company.
</Tip>
