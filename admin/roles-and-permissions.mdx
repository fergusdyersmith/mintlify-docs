CTRL keeps roles deliberately simple: two assignable roles, plus positions derived from reporting lines.

## The two roles

- **Owner** — holds every company permission: admin pages, company objectives, teams, people, invitations, and cycles. The founding owner can't be demoted or removed.
- **Member** — the default for everyone invited. No admin permissions.

Change a person's role in **Admin → People**. Invitations always create Members; promote to Owner afterwards if needed. Owners can also demote other owners back to Member — except the founding owner, who is flagged as primary and can't be demoted or removed.

## Derived positions (not settings)

- **Manager** — anyone with at least one direct report. Grants the Team Dashboard, access to reports' check-ins, and the pen on their OKRs.
- **Top level** — someone with no manager. Self-serves their own OKRs.
- **CEO** — a per-company flag with special dashboard behaviour. See [The CEO flag](/admin/ceo-flag).

## The capability matrix

| Capability | Member | Manager | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Own dashboard, check-in, OKR view | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Create/edit own OKRs | Only if no manager | Only if no manager | Only if no manager |
| Set OKRs for direct reports | — | ✅ | ✅ (anyone) |
| Team Dashboard & reports' check-ins | — | ✅ | ✅ (all) |
| Company Dashboard & company objectives | — | — | ✅ |
| Admin: Teams, People, Cycle, Settings | — | — | ✅ |

<Note>
  What colleagues can _see_ of each other's OKRs on the Company page is a separate per-person setting — see [Visibility controls](/admin/visibility-controls).
</Note>
