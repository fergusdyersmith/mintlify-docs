In **Admin → Settings**, owners can customise the **check-in reminder email** — the message people receive the morning their weekly check-in is due.

## Editing the template

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Admin → Settings">
    Find the **Email templates** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit subject and body">
    The default subject is _"Your weekly check-in is due today."_ Personalise with placeholders:

    - `:name` — the recipient's name
    - `:company` — your company name
    - `:week` — the current cycle week
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save email templates">
    The next reminders sent use your wording.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the reminder behaves

- Sent in the morning (around 09:00) on each person's due day — only if they haven't submitted, and at most once per check-in.
- Includes a **Complete your check-in** button that opens the check-in wizard directly.
- Individuals can switch reminders off for themselves — see [Reminders](/check-ins/reminders).

<Note>
  Invitation emails are sent automatically through CTRL's sign-in provider when you invite someone, and aren't customisable here.
</Note>
