The [Company page](/dashboard/company-page) lists everyone in the company — but what each viewer sees of their _colleagues'_ OKRs is up to you. **Admin → Settings → People page visibility** holds two per-person switches:

- **Can view others' objectives** — whether this person sees colleagues' objectives when browsing the Company page.
- **Can view others' key results** — whether they also see the key results underneath.

Everyone **always sees their own** OKRs in full, and managers/owners always see their people's regardless of these flags.

## Bulk actions

Two shortcuts set the whole company at once:

- **Make all objectives visible**
- **Make key results visible**

Use the per-person matrix underneath for exceptions.

## Choosing a policy

- **Full transparency** (both on for everyone) — the default CTRL posture; the Company page becomes a real map of who's driving what.
- **Objectives only** — colleagues see direction but not the metrics; useful where targets are sensitive.
- **Neither** — the directory still shows people, titles, and teams, but OKR detail stays within reporting lines.

<Note>
  These flags only affect the browsing view on the Company page — enforcement is server-side, and reporting-line access (manager, owner) is unaffected. See [Roles and permissions](/admin/roles-and-permissions).
</Note>
