**Admin → People** is the master list of everyone in your company — and the place to change almost anything about them.

## Views and filters

Toggle between **List** and [**Org chart**](/admin/org-chart). Filter the list by team or manager, or show only people with **No manager**.

## Per-person actions

From a person's row you can:

- Edit their **name**, **job title**, **role summary**, and uploaded **documents** (CVs / job descriptions).
- Upload or remove their **profile photo** (JPG/PNG/WebP up to 4 MB).
- Change their **Team**.
- Change their **Manager** — this is what reporting lines, Team Dashboards, and OKR-setting rights are built on.
- Change their **role** — Owner or Member. See [Roles and permissions](/admin/roles-and-permissions).
- Mark them as **Chief / CEO** — one per company. See [The CEO flag](/admin/ceo-flag).
- **Remove from company**.

## Adding people

- **One at a time** — the **Add person** dialog. See [Inviting people](/admin/inviting-people).
- **In bulk** — spreadsheet import with preview. See [Importing people](/admin/importing-people).

## Pending invitations

Invitations that haven't been accepted yet are listed with a cancel action. Invitations expire after **7 days** — re-invite anyone who missed theirs.

<Note>
  Removing someone from the company is immediate and affects their reports (they lose their manager). Reassign reporting lines afterwards.
</Note>
