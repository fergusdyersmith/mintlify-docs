**Admin → OKR Cycle** sets the cadence the whole company runs on — as the page puts it, _"the cadence for OKR planning and weekly check-ins."_

## Current cycle — where things stand

The top of the page shows the live cycle: its label (e.g. **Q3 2026**), **Week X of Y**, a progress bar, and the start date.

## Cadence & check-in settings

- **Cycle length** — **Quarterly**, **Trimester**, **Bi-Annual**, or **Annual**. This drives cycle labels (Q3 2026, T2 2026, H1 2026…) and the number of weeks per cycle.
- **Cycle start date** — when the current cycle began; week numbering counts from here.
- **Default check-in day** and **Default check-in time** — when weekly check-ins fall due for everyone who hasn't set a personal override (out of the box: **Friday 17:00**).

Click **Save cycle settings** to apply.

## Notes on changing settings mid-cycle

- Changing the default day/time affects everyone using the company default from the next due date; people with a personal slot (**Settings → Notifications**) keep theirs. See [Check-in schedule](/check-ins/schedule).
- Changing length or start date re-frames week numbering — do it deliberately, ideally at a cycle boundary.

<Note>
  Moving to the _next_ cycle isn't a setting — it's the **Start new cycle** action. See [Starting a new cycle](/admin/starting-a-new-cycle).
</Note>
