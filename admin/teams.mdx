**Admin → Teams** manages the containers that group people for dashboards and roll-ups.

## Creating a team

Click **Create a team**, give it a name, and optionally pick a **Team leader**. Each team card shows its **OKR progress** and member count.

You can also create a team inline wherever a team picker appears (for example while adding a person) — use **New team**, name it, and you're returned to what you were doing.

## The team detail page

Open a team to manage it:

- **Roster** — who's in the team; **Add a member** or **Remove** people.
- **Rename** the team.
- **Set or clear the leader**.
- **Delete** the team (people aren't deleted — they're just no longer grouped).

## Leader vs manager — the distinction that matters

As the UI puts it: _"Managers are derived from reporting lines on the People page. Lead is an optional cosmetic badge."_

- A **manager** is whoever people report to — that's what grants a Team Dashboard and OKR-setting rights. Set reporting lines in [Admin → People](/admin/people).
- A **team leader** is a label on the team card. It grants **no permissions**.

So if someone "leads a team" but can't see a Team Dashboard, the fix is reporting lines, not the leader badge.

<Note>
  People also inherit a team automatically when a manager adds them to their team from the Team Dashboard — see [Adding team members](/managers/adding-team-members).
</Note>
