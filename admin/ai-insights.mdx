Instead of reading every check-in, owners get two AI-generated panels on the **Company Dashboard**:

## Feedback themes — "What people are talking about"

Overarching themes pulled from everyone's check-in **notes** (the "Anything else?" step). Each theme has a title, a short summary, a sentiment, and a count of how many check-ins mentioned it.

## Blocker themes — "What's getting in the way"

Recurring **blockers** grouped across all check-ins — the patterns behind the individual "waiting on X" entries.

## How they work

- Themes are generated per company per cycle and **cached** — the panels load what was last generated.
- Click **Refresh insights** to regenerate both panels from the latest check-ins.
- More check-ins in, better themes out — thin weeks produce thin insights.

<Note>
  These panels depend on your company's AI configuration. If AI isn't configured, they simply stay empty — the rest of the dashboard is unaffected.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Refresh after most check-ins land (e.g. Friday evening or Monday morning), and treat a theme with a high mention count as an agenda item for leadership, not just an observation.
</Tip>
