Invite one person at a time with **Add person** on **Admin → People** (for many at once, use [Importing people](/admin/importing-people)).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add person">
    On **Admin → People**, click **Add person**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in their details">
    - **Name** and **Email** — the invitation goes to this address.
    - **Job title**
    - **Team** and **Manager** — so they land in the right place on day one.
    - **Role summary** and/or a **CV / job description** upload — the form asks you to provide at least one; this becomes their [role profile](/managers/role-profiles) and powers AI OKR suggestions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send invite">
    CTRL emails a one-click invitation. When the person signs in with that email, the invitation auto-accepts and everything you entered is applied.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After sending

- The invite appears under **Pending invitations**, where you can cancel it.
- Invitations **expire after 7 days** — send a fresh one if it lapses.
- Once accepted, the person appears in the People list and on their manager's Team Dashboard.

<Note>
  The new joiner's experience of this flow is documented in [Accepting an invitation](/getting-started/accepting-an-invitation) — handy to link in your welcome message.
</Note>
