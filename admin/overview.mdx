Company **Owners** run CTRL from two places: the **Company Dashboard** (the live picture) and the **Admin** group in the sidebar (the controls).

## The Admin pages

- **Teams** — create teams, manage rosters, and mark team leaders. See [Managing teams](/admin/teams).
- **People** — the heart of admin: add and invite people, bulk-import from a spreadsheet, set managers and teams, view the org chart, and manage pending invitations. See [Managing people](/admin/people).
- **OKR Cycle** — set cycle length, start date, and the default check-in day and time; start new cycles. See [Cycle settings](/admin/cycle-settings).
- **Settings** — customise the check-in reminder email and control OKR visibility on the Company page. See [Email templates](/admin/email-templates) and [Visibility controls](/admin/visibility-controls).

## The Company Dashboard

Cycle pace, check-in completion, open blockers, company objectives, per-team progress, mood, and AI-generated themes — see [The Company Dashboard](/admin/company-dashboard).

## Setting up from scratch?

Follow the full [Onboarding your company](/workflows/onboarding-your-company) workflow — it sequences these pages in the right order.

<Note>
  Owners hold every permission; regular members hold none of the admin ones. The full matrix is in [Roles and permissions](/admin/roles-and-permissions).
</Note>
