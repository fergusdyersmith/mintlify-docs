Check-ins replace the "any updates?" meeting. Here's how to review a week's worth in a few minutes.

## The three levels of detail

1. **Team Dashboard** — the scan. Who's checked in, each person's mood, and a **Blocked** / **No blockers** tag per row (hover **Blocked** for the full text). Start here.
2. **Member profile** — the trend. One person's OKRs plus their recent check-ins for the cycle, with mood and blocker per week. See [Member profiles](/managers/member-profiles).
3. **Check-in detail** — the full record: **Blocker**, **Mood this week**, the **Key results updated** table (update, status, and notes per key result), and **"In their words"** notes. See [Viewing past check-ins](/check-ins/viewing-past-check-ins).

## A weekly review routine

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check completion first">
    The **Checked in this week** KPI tells you whether the data is trustworthy. Nudge anyone missing — their reminder may be off or their due day later than you think.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Triage blockers">
    Read every **Blocked** tag. For each: can you unblock it, escalate it, or park it explicitly?
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scan moods">
    Anyone **Struggling** deserves a conversation this week, not at the next review. Watch for slides from **Great/Good** toward **Neutral**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spot-check key results">
    Open the detail for anyone whose OKRs went **At risk** or **Off track** and read their notes for the why.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  There's no reply or comment feature on check-ins — the follow-up happens in your 1:1 or wherever your team talks. CTRL's job is to make sure you walk in knowing what to talk about.
</Note>
