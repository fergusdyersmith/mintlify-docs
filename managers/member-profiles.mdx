Click any report on your Team Dashboard to open their **member profile** — the one-page view of how that person's cycle is going.

## What's on it

- **Their OKRs** for the current cycle, with key results, statuses, and progress.
- **Recent check-ins** — the last several check-ins of the cycle, each showing the week, mood, and **Blocker**. Click through for the [full check-in detail](/check-ins/viewing-past-check-ins).
- **Add OKR** — create a new objective for this person. See [Setting OKRs for your team](/managers/setting-team-okrs).

## Who can see a member profile

The person themselves, their manager, and company owners. Peers can't browse each other's profiles — the transparent surface for colleagues is the [Company page](/dashboard/company-page), which respects visibility settings.

<Tip>
  This page is built for 1:1 prep: OKR health on top, the last few weeks of moods and blockers underneath — the agenda writes itself.
</Tip>
