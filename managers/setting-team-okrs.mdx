As a manager you hold the pen on your reports' OKRs — see [Who sets whose OKRs](/okrs/who-sets-okrs). This is the workflow at the start of each cycle (or whenever someone joins).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start from the company objectives">
    Open the [Company page](/dashboard/company-page) and re-read where the company is heading this cycle. Your team's OKRs should ladder into these — and into your own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check role profiles">
    Sharp [role profiles](/managers/role-profiles) make the next step much better — a role summary or CV per person is enough.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Draft with AI, per person">
    Open each report's [member profile](/managers/member-profiles) → **Add OKR** → **Suggest with AI**. The AI grounds its drafts in the company objectives, your OKRs, and the person's role profile. See [AI OKR suggestions](/okrs/ai-suggestions).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Discuss in a 1:1">
    Treat the draft as an agenda, not a verdict — agree the objectives and targets together.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and sanity-check">
    Save each OKR (see [Creating an OKR](/okrs/creating-an-okr)), then glance at your Team Dashboard: every person should have OKRs, and their key results now flow into weekly check-ins automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  OKRs don't carry over between cycles — when a new cycle starts, every report needs a fresh set. See [Starting a new cycle](/admin/starting-a-new-cycle).
</Note>
