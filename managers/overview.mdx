In CTRL, "manager" isn't a role you're granted — it's derived from reporting lines. The moment someone reports to you, you get the **Team Dashboard** and the responsibility for their OKRs.

## What you can do as a manager

- **See the Team Dashboard** — progress, check-ins, blockers, and mood across your reports. See [The Team Dashboard](/managers/team-dashboard).
- **Build your team** — add people who don't yet have a manager, and edit their profiles. See [Adding team members](/managers/adding-team-members).
- **Set your reports' OKRs** — you hold the pen for their objectives each cycle. See [Setting OKRs for your team](/managers/setting-team-okrs).
- **Read their check-ins** — each report's weekly submission, in full. See [Reviewing check-ins](/managers/reviewing-check-ins).
- **Maintain role profiles** — the role summaries and CVs that make AI OKR suggestions sharp. See [Role profiles](/managers/role-profiles).

## The weekly rhythm

1. Your reports check in (each on their own due day).
2. You scan the **Team Dashboard**: who's checked in, who's blocked, how moods look.
3. You act on blockers and follow up with anyone **Struggling** — ideally before your 1:1s.

<Note>
  Your own manager (or the company owner) sees your check-ins the same way you see your team's — the loop is the same at every level.
</Note>
