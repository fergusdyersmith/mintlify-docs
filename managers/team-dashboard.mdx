The **Team Dashboard** appears in your sidebar as soon as at least one person reports to you.

## Top of the page

Four KPIs summarise the week: **Avg progress**, **Checked in this week**, **Open blockers**, and **People**.

## The team list

One row per report, showing their avatar, OKR progress, latest check-in mood, and a **Blocked** / **No blockers** tag — hover a **Blocked** tag to read the full blocker text. From a row's menu you can edit the member's profile or **Remove** them from your team. Click through to their [member profile](/managers/member-profiles) for detail.

## The charts

- **This week at a glance** — check-in completion.
- **Status — Where each OKR sits** — the On track / At risk / Off track / Done mix.
- **Progress distribution** — how progress spreads across the team.
- **Mood this week — Sentiment mix** and **Weighted team sentiment, 1–10** — the team's emotional temperature.
- **Team average vs target** — progress against the on-pace line.

## Reading it in 60 seconds

1. **Checked in this week** low? Chase check-ins first — everything else on the page is only as fresh as they are.
2. Scan **Blocked** tags — unblocking people is the highest-leverage manager move in CTRL.
3. Glance at the mood mix — a slide towards **Neutral**/**Struggling** is your early warning.
