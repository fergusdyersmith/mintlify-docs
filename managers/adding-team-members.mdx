## Adding a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Add a member">
    On the **Team Dashboard**, click **Add a member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick someone">
    The list only offers people who **don't yet have a manager**. If the person you want isn't listed, they already report to someone — an owner can change their manager in **Admin → People**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    The person now reports to you and inherits your team. They appear on your Team Dashboard immediately, and you can set their OKRs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Removing a member

Use **Remove** from the person's row menu on the Team Dashboard. This clears the reporting line — it does **not** remove them from the company (that's an owner action in **Admin → People**). Until someone else picks them up, they're manager-less and can set their own OKRs.

## What changes when someone joins your team

- You gain access to their check-ins, and they show in your roll-ups.
- **You** now hold the pen on their OKRs — see [Setting OKRs for your team](/managers/setting-team-okrs).
- They inherit your **team** grouping for dashboards.

<Note>
  Owners can also set managers and teams for anyone at once in **Admin → People** — handy when restructuring. See [Managing people](/admin/people).
</Note>
