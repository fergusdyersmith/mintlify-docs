Each person in CTRL has a **role profile**: their job title, a **role summary** describing what the role is responsible for, and optionally uploaded **CVs / job descriptions**. Its main job is to ground [AI OKR suggestions](/okrs/ai-suggestions) — the better the profile, the sharper the drafts.

## Editing a report's role profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the edit panel">
    On the **Team Dashboard**, open the row menu for the person and choose edit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the role">
    - **Title** — e.g. _"Product Designer"_.
    - **Role summary** — what this role is responsible for; a few sentences is plenty.
    - **CVs / job descriptions** — upload PDF, Word, or text files (up to 5 MB each).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The profile is used the next time anyone opens **Suggest with AI** for this person.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who else maintains role profiles

- **The person themselves** — in **Settings → Profile** under **Role profile**. See [Your role profile](/settings/role-profile).
- **Admins** — when adding a person in **Admin → People**, the invite form asks for a role summary or CV up front, so the profile exists before the person even joins.

<Tip>
  A good role summary reads like the first line of a job ad: _"Owns activation and onboarding — responsible for new-user conversion and time-to-value."_
</Tip>
