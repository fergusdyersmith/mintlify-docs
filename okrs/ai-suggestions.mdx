When creating an OKR, click **Suggest with AI** to have CTRL draft objectives and key results instead of starting from a blank form.

## What the AI is grounded on

Suggestions aren't generic — they're built from:

- The **company objectives** for the current cycle.
- The **manager's own OKRs** (so suggestions ladder into them).
- The person's **role profile** — their role summary and any uploaded CV or job description.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Suggest with AI">
    From the Add OKR form, click **Suggest with AI**. The dialog is titled _"Suggest OKRs for_ {name}_"_.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optionally steer it">
    Use _"Anything specific to ladder into (optional)?"_ to point the AI at a particular goal or theme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate and pick">
    Click **Generate**. Not happy? **Re-generate** for a fresh set. Click **Use this** on any suggestion to prefill the form (it flips to **Added**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit and save">
    Suggestions are drafts — adjust wording, types, and targets, then **Save OKR**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Getting sharper suggestions

If you see _"No role profile yet"_, add a **role summary** or upload a CV first — either on the person's profile (managers/admins) or in **Settings → Profile** (yourself). See [Your role profile](/settings/role-profile).

<Note>
  AI features depend on your company's AI configuration. If generation isn't available, the form still works normally — write OKRs by hand.
</Note>
