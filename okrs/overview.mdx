An **OKR** pairs an ambitious, qualitative **objective** ("Ship the new onboarding flow") with measurable **key results** that prove you got there. In CTRL, OKRs live inside the current [cycle](/getting-started/key-concepts#cycle) and are refreshed every week by check-ins.

## Three levels of OKRs

- **Company objectives** — set by owners on the Company Dashboard. One line, ambitious, qualitative — the direction everything else ladders into. See [Company objectives](/admin/company-objectives).
- **Exec OKRs** — the CEO's personal OKRs, driven directly by the company objectives.
- **Personal OKRs** — everyone else's, usually set by their manager. See [Who sets whose OKRs](/okrs/who-sets-okrs).

## Anatomy of an OKR

- **Objective** — the one-line goal.
- **Context (optional)** — why it matters this cycle.
- **Key results** (typically 2\+) — each with a measurable result, a [type](/okrs/key-result-types) (Percent, Scale 1–10, or Status), a **target**, and a live [status](/okrs/statuses).

## How OKRs stay current

There's no separate "update OKRs" chore: the weekly check-in's Progress step writes fresh values and statuses onto your key results. Dashboards then aggregate everything — see [How progress is calculated](/okrs/progress-calculation).
