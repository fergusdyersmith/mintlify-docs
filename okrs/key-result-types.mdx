Every key result is measured one of three ways. Pick the type when you create it; it shapes what the owner enters at every weekly check-in.

## Percent

A percentage against a target — best for anything with a natural % measure: activation rate, coverage, completion. Progress is **current ÷ target**, capped at 100%.

## Scale 1–10

A score from 1 to 10 against a target — useful for survey scores, quality ratings, or judgement calls you want to track numerically. Progress works the same way: **current ÷ target**, capped at 100%.

## Status

No number at all — the key result is simply in a state: **On track**, **At risk**, **Off track**, or **Done**. Best for deliverables that are essentially done-or-not: _"Contract signed"_, _"Feature shipped"_. For progress purposes, **Done** counts as 100%, **On track** as 50%, and anything else as 0%.

## Choosing well

- If you can count it, use **Percent** (or **Scale** for scores) — numeric key results give the smoothest progress tracking.
- Use **Status** sparingly: its progress jumps in coarse steps, which makes objective progress lumpier.
- Set targets that are ambitious but reachable — progress caps at 100%, so there's no bonus for sandbagging.
