CTRL follows the reporting line: **your manager sets your OKRs**. The full rules:

| OKRs for… | Who can create/edit them |
| --- | --- |
| **A person with a manager** | Their manager (and company owners) |
| **A person with no manager** | Themselves — top-level people self-serve |
| **The CEO** | Themselves (their "exec OKRs" ladder into company objectives) |
| **Company objectives** | Company owners only |

## What this looks like in the app

- If your manager sets your OKRs, your dashboard shows _"Your manager will set your OKRs for this cycle"_ instead of an **Add OKR** button.
- Managers add OKRs from a team member's profile — **Add an OKR for** {name}. See [Setting OKRs for your team](/managers/setting-team-okrs).
- Owners can manage OKRs for anyone in the company.

## Why it works this way

OKR-setting is treated as a conversation between you and your manager — the manager holds the pen so that goals ladder upwards consistently. Discuss them in a 1:1; the manager records the outcome (often starting from an [AI suggestion](/okrs/ai-suggestions)).

<Note>
  Separately, per-person visibility flags control who can _see_ other people's OKRs on the Company page — see [Visibility controls](/admin/visibility-controls).
</Note>
