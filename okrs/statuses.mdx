Every key result carries one of four statuses, updated at each weekly check-in:

| Status | Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **On track** | Green | Progressing as expected |
| **At risk** | Amber | Might miss without a change |
| **Off track** | Red | Will miss without intervention |
| **Done** | Grey | Complete |

## Where statuses appear

- On each key result on your **Personal Dashboard**, and rolled up into pills counting objectives **at risk** / **off track**.
- On the **Team Dashboard**'s _Status — where each OKR sits_ chart, so managers see the health mix across the team.
- On the **Company** page and **Company Dashboard** roll-ups.
- Your OKR list is sorted by health — **at-risk first** — so trouble is always at the top.

## Using them honestly

- **At risk** is an early warning, not an admission of failure — flag it the week you first doubt the target.
- Pair a red or amber status with a [blocker](/check-ins/reporting-blockers) so your manager knows _why_.
- Statuses also feed progress for Status-type key results — see [How progress is calculated](/okrs/progress-calculation).
