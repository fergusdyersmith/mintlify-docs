CTRL (PlatformCTRL in full) is a performance management tool built around two simple habits:

1. **Everyone has OKRs** — a few objectives with measurable key results, set for each cycle (usually a quarter).
2. **Everyone checks in weekly** — a ~2-minute update covering key-result progress, your biggest blocker, your mood, and anything else worth sharing.

Those two habits feed three dashboards: a **Personal Dashboard** for your own OKRs and check-in, a **Team Dashboard** for anyone with direct reports, and a **Company Dashboard** for owners — plus a transparent **Company** directory where you can see what everyone is working towards.

You can reach this help centre any time from inside the app — click **Help** at the bottom of the sidebar.
