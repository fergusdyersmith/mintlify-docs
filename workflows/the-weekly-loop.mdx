Everything in CTRL runs on one seven-day loop. Here's the journey of a single week, across all three altitudes.

## 1. The nudge

On the morning a check-in is due, CTRL emails the person (_"Your weekly check-in is due today"_) with a **Complete your check-in** button, and drops a matching in-app notification. See [Reminders](/check-ins/reminders).

## 2. The check-in (≈2 minutes)

The person walks the four steps — **Progress → Blockers → Mood → Notes** ([Submitting a check-in](/check-ins/submitting)). On submit:

- The fresh values and statuses are written straight onto their **key results** — this is how OKR progress moves.
- The blocker is compressed by AI into a short headline for scanning.
- Miss the due time and an overdue notification appears instead.

## 3. The manager's scan

On the **Team Dashboard**, the manager sees who's checked in, each person's mood, and **Blocked** tags — and triages: unblock, follow up with anyone **Struggling**, spot-check At-risk OKRs. The routine is in [Reviewing check-ins](/managers/reviewing-check-ins).

## 4. The company view

On the **Company Dashboard**, the same week rolls up for owners: **Checked in**, **Open blockers**, **Pace** against the cycle, per-team progress, and the mood mix. Clicking **Refresh insights** regenerates the two AI panels — **Feedback themes** and **Blocker themes** — from the week's notes and blockers. See [AI insights](/admin/ai-insights).

## 5. The conversation

CTRL doesn't host the follow-up — the 1:1s, the unblocking, the leadership decisions happen wherever your team talks. Its job is to make sure those conversations start from the same, current picture.

<Tip>
  The loop is only as good as its weakest step: completion. If **Checked in this week** slips, fix that before reading anything else on the dashboards.
</Tip>
