This is the full setup sequence for a new company on CTRL. Done in this order, each step feeds the next; expect the whole thing to take an hour plus however long invitations take to be accepted.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the cycle before anything else">
    In **Admin → OKR Cycle**, choose your **cycle length** (Quarterly is the common choice), the **cycle start date**, and the **default check-in day and time**. Everything else — week numbering, due dates, dashboards — hangs off this. See [Cycle settings](/admin/cycle-settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your teams">
    In **Admin → Teams**, create the teams you'll group people into. See [Managing teams](/admin/teams).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite everyone">
    For a handful of people, use **Add person** ([Inviting people](/admin/inviting-people)); for the whole company, use the spreadsheet import with the **Manager email** column filled in — it builds your reporting lines automatically ([Importing people](/admin/importing-people)). Include role summaries or CVs where you can: they power AI OKR suggestions later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the org chart">
    Once invitations are accepted, open **Admin → People → Org chart** and fix anyone flagged _"Not connected to the top of the chart"_. Reporting lines are what make Team Dashboards and OKR-setting work. See [The org chart](/admin/org-chart).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark the CEO and any extra owners">
    In **Admin → People**, set the **Chief / CEO** flag ([The CEO flag](/admin/ceo-flag)) and promote anyone else who should administer CTRL to **Owner** ([Roles and permissions](/admin/roles-and-permissions)).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set company objectives">
    On the **Company Dashboard**, create three to five [company objectives](/admin/company-objectives) — the goals everyone's OKRs will ladder into.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cascade OKRs">
    The CEO sets their exec OKRs; each manager sets OKRs for their reports (the [Setting OKRs for your team](/managers/setting-team-okrs) workflow); top-level people add their own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Decide visibility and tune the reminder email">
    In **Admin → Company Settings**, choose your transparency policy ([Visibility controls](/admin/visibility-controls)) and optionally reword the reminder email ([Email templates](/admin/email-templates)).
  </Step>
</Steps>

After the first check-in day passes, the dashboards come alive — from here the company runs on [the weekly loop](/workflows/the-weekly-loop).
