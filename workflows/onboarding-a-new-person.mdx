Three people make onboarding work: the **admin** who invites, the **new joiner** who accepts, and the **manager** who sets OKRs. Here's the whole relay.

## Stage 1 — Admin: invite with a full profile

In **Admin → People → Add person**, enter their name, email, job title, **team**, and **manager**, plus a **role summary** or **CV** (the form asks for at least one). Then **Send invite**. Everything you enter is applied automatically when they join — details in [Inviting people](/admin/inviting-people).

<Note>
  Invitations expire after **7 days** — watch **Pending invitations** and re-send if needed.
</Note>

## Stage 2 — New joiner: accept and settle in

They click **Accept invitation** in the email, sign in, and land on their Personal Dashboard with team, manager, and profile already in place ([Accepting an invitation](/getting-started/accepting-an-invitation)). Their first-day checklist — photo, check-in slot, a look around — is [Your first week](/getting-started/your-first-week).

## Stage 3 — Manager: set their OKRs

The new joiner's dashboard says _"Your manager will set your OKRs for this cycle"_ until this happens. Open their [member profile](/managers/member-profiles) → **Add OKR**, and lean on **Suggest with AI** — it uses the role profile the admin captured at invite time ([AI OKR suggestions](/okrs/ai-suggestions)). Agree the goals in your first 1:1.

## Stage 4 — The loop begins

On their first due day they'll get the reminder email and submit their first [check-in](/check-ins/submitting) — and they're fully onboard.

| Who | Does | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Admin | Invite with profile, team, manager | Admin → People |
| New joiner | Accept, set photo & check-in slot | Email → Settings |
| Manager | Set OKRs, first 1:1 | Member profile |
| Everyone | Weekly check-ins | Dashboard |
