---

**Settings → Appearance** has one setting: **Colour mode**.

- **Light** — always light.
- **Dark** — always dark.
- **System** — follow your operating system's setting, switching automatically when it does.

The choice is saved **per device**, so your laptop and phone can differ.

<Note>
  Appearance is purely personal — it doesn't change anything for anyone else in your company.
</Note>
