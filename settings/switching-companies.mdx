If you belong to more than one company on CTRL (a fractional exec, a coach, or someone mid-transition), a **company switcher** appears in the sidebar.

## How it works

- Pick a company from the switcher; every page — dashboards, check-ins, OKRs, admin — now shows that company's data.
- Each company is fully separate: its own cycle, OKRs, check-ins, teams, and roles. Your role can differ per company (Owner in one, Member in another).
- When you sign in, CTRL lands you in one of your companies; use the switcher to move.

## What stays per-company

Everything work-related: your OKRs, check-in schedule and history, team, manager, and notifications all belong to the company they were created in. Submitting a check-in in one company doesn't touch the other.

<Note>
  If you expect to see a company and it's missing from the switcher, your invitation may be pending or expired — ask that company's admin to check **Pending invitations**.
</Note>
