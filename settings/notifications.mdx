**Settings → Notifications** controls your reminders and your personal check-in slot — as the page puts it, _"Choose your reminders and set the default slot for your weekly check-in."_

## Reminder toggles

- **Weekly check-in reminder** — the email (plus in-app notification) sent the morning your check-in is due. See [Reminders](/check-ins/reminders).
- **Overdue check-in reminder** — the in-app notification raised if your due time passes without a submission.

Both are on by default; switch off whichever you don't want.

## Your check-in slot

- **Your check-in day** and **Your check-in time** — override the company default with a slot that fits your week.
- Tick **Use company default** to follow the company-wide setting (set by your admin — see [Cycle settings](/admin/cycle-settings)).

Click **Save preferences** to apply. Your dashboard's due tag and your reminder emails follow the new slot from the next check-in.

<Tip>
  Pick a slot you'll actually honour — end of your focused workweek is ideal. Friday-afternoon check-ins written in two minutes beat Monday back-fill from memory.
</Tip>
