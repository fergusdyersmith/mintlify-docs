**Settings → Profile** (from your avatar menu) holds your personal information.

## Profile information

- **Full name** — how you appear across dashboards and the Company page.
- **Email address** — where invitations, reminders, and sign-in are tied.
- **Profile photo** — upload a JPG, PNG, or WebP up to 4 MB, or **Remove current photo** to fall back to your initials.

## Role profile

The same page holds your **role summary** and uploaded **CVs / job descriptions** — the inputs that make [AI OKR suggestions](/okrs/ai-suggestions) specific to your job. It matters enough to have its own page: [Your role profile](/settings/role-profile).

## Account deletion

CTRL uses a hosted sign-in provider for accounts, so account deletion is handled through it rather than a button in Settings — contact your company admin.

<Note>
  Your job title, team, and manager are managed by your admin in **Admin → People**, not here — ask them if those need correcting.
</Note>
