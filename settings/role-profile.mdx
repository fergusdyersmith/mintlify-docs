Your **role profile** — in **Settings → Profile** — is what [AI OKR suggestions](/okrs/ai-suggestions) read to understand your job. Without it, suggestions fall back to company-level context; with it, they get specific.

## What's in it

- **Role summary** — a few sentences on what your role is responsible for. Think first-line-of-a-job-ad: _"Owns the mid-market sales pipeline — responsible for new ARR and win rate."_
- **CVs / job descriptions** — upload PDF, Word, or text files up to 5 MB each. Files can be downloaded or removed later.

Click **Save role profile** after editing.

## Who else can edit it

- **Your manager** — from your row on their Team Dashboard. See [Role profiles](/managers/role-profiles).
- **Admins** — in **Admin → People**, and usually at invite time (the Add person form asks for a role summary or CV up front).

## When it's used

Whenever anyone opens **Suggest with AI** for your OKRs, the AI grounds its drafts on the company objectives, your manager's OKRs, and this profile. If it's empty, the dialog notes _"No role profile yet"_ — your cue to fill this in.

<Tip>
  A job description beats a CV here — the AI cares about what the role is accountable for now, not your career history.
</Tip>
