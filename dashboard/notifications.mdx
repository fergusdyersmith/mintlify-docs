## The bell

The bell icon in the top bar shows your unread count and the ten most recent notifications. When there's nothing new it reads **"You are all caught up."** Click **View all notifications** to open the full page.

## The Notifications page

The **Activity** list shows every notification, with an **Unread** badge on new ones. You can **Mark read** individually or mark everything read at once.

## What CTRL notifies you about

- **"Your weekly check-in is due today"** — arrives the morning your check-in is due, paired with a reminder email.
- **"Your weekly check-in is overdue"** — appears in-app if your due time passes without a submission.

Both can be switched off individually in **Settings → Notifications** — see [Notification preferences](/settings/notifications).

<Note>
  CTRL is deliberately quiet: notifications exist to protect the weekly check-in habit, not to create noise.
</Note>
