The **Company** entry in the sidebar opens the company directory — CTRL's transparency page.

## What's on it

- **Company objectives** — where the company is heading this cycle, with overall progress and an on-pace marker showing how far through the cycle you are.
- **Everyone** — an alphabetical list of every member with their photo, name, job title, team, and objective count. Expand a row to see that person's objectives and key results.

## What you can see

What's visible when you expand a colleague depends on visibility settings your admin controls:

- Whether you can see other people's **objectives**.
- Whether you can also see their **key results**.

You always see your own in full. If a colleague's row looks thin, that's the visibility setting, not a missing feature — see [Visibility controls](/admin/visibility-controls).

<Tip>
  Use this page before planning or cross-team work: two minutes of scanning what other teams are aiming at prevents most duplicated effort.
</Tip>
