The **Personal Dashboard** is where you land after signing in. It answers three questions: what's due this week, what are my OKRs, and how are they doing?

## What's on it

- **Header** — a greeting, your job title, and pills counting objectives **at risk** or **off track**.
- **"This week" banner** — your check-in due date (_Due today / Due tomorrow / Due in N days_), **Week X of Y** of the cycle, how many objectives and key results you have to update, and the **Submit weekly check-in** button. After you submit it reads **"You are all caught up."**
- **My OKRs** — your objectives for the current cycle, sorted by health (at-risk first), each with its key results, statuses, and progress.

## What you can do here

- **Submit your weekly check-in** — opens the four-step flow in a modal. See [Submitting a check-in](/check-ins/submitting).
- **Add or edit OKRs** — only if you have no manager (or are the CEO); otherwise your manager sets them and you'll see _"Your manager will set your OKRs for this cycle."_ See [Who sets whose OKRs](/okrs/who-sets-okrs).

## If you're the CEO

Your dashboard shows a read-only **Company objectives** block above your own OKRs, and your own section is labelled **My exec OKRs** — your goals are expected to ladder directly into the company's. See [The CEO flag](/admin/ceo-flag).
