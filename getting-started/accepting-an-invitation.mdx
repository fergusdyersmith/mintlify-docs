When an admin adds you to a company, CTRL sends an invitation to your work email. Accepting takes one click.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invitation email">
    Look for the invitation to join your company on CTRL. Click **Accept invitation**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    The link takes you through CTRL's secure sign-in with the same email address. There's no password to create — see [Signing in](/getting-started/signing-in).
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    The invitation is accepted automatically and you land on your Personal Dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What comes with your invitation

Admins usually prepare your profile before inviting you. On acceptance, CTRL applies:

- Your **name** and **job title**
- Your **team** and **manager** — so you appear on the right Team Dashboard immediately
- Your **role summary** and any uploaded **CV / job description** — used later to tailor AI OKR suggestions

## Good to know

- Invitations **expire after 7 days**. If yours has lapsed, ask your admin to re-send it — they can see pending invitations in **Admin → People**.
- The invitation is tied to the email address it was sent to; sign in with that address.
- Once you've joined, your manager typically sets your OKRs for the cycle — see [Who sets whose OKRs](/okrs/who-sets-okrs).
