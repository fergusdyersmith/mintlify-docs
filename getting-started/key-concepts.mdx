Five concepts explain almost everything in CTRL.

## Cycle

The repeating planning period your company runs on — **Quarterly**, **Trimester**, **Bi-Annual**, or **Annual**. OKRs and check-ins belong to a cycle (labelled e.g. **Q3 2026**), and dashboards show **Week X of Y** of the current one. When a cycle ends, an admin starts a new one and everyone sets fresh OKRs.

## Objectives & key results (OKRs)

An **objective** is a one-line, ambitious, qualitative goal. Each objective has **key results** — measurable outcomes tracked as a **percent**, a **1–10 scale**, or a simple **status**. Each key result carries a health status: **On track**, **At risk**, **Off track**, or **Done**. There are personal OKRs (everyone), exec OKRs (the CEO), and **company objectives** (set by owners).

## Weekly check-in

A ≈2-minute weekly update — **Progress → Blockers → Mood → Notes** — that refreshes your key results and tells your manager how you're doing. See [Weekly check-ins](/check-ins/overview).

## Teams & managers

**Teams** group people for dashboards and roll-ups. Your **manager** is whoever you report to — reporting lines are what give someone a Team Dashboard and the ability to set your OKRs. A team can also have a **leader** badge, which is cosmetic; real authority comes from reporting lines.

## Roles

- **Member** — the default: does check-ins, sees their own OKRs and the Company directory.
- **Manager** — not a setting, but anyone with direct reports: gains the Team Dashboard.
- **Owner** — runs the company in CTRL: admin pages, company objectives, cycles, people.

Full details in [Roles and permissions](/admin/roles-and-permissions) — and [Who sees what](/getting-started/who-sees-what) shows how each role changes the screens you see.
