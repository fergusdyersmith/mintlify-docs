CTRL doesn't store passwords. Sign-in is handled by a secure hosted login (WorkOS AuthKit): go to your CTRL site, click **Log in**, and authenticate with your work email.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open CTRL and click Log in">
    The welcome page has a single **Log in** button.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authenticate with your work email">
    You're taken to the hosted sign-in page. Use the same email address your invitation was sent to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on your dashboard">
    After signing in you're taken straight to your Personal Dashboard for your company.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## First sign-in with a pending invitation

If you were invited to a company, signing in with the invited email address accepts the invitation automatically — your job title, team, manager, and any role profile your admin prepared are applied to your account. See [Accepting an invitation](/getting-started/accepting-an-invitation).

## Troubleshooting

- **"I signed in but see no company"** — you're signed in with a different email address than the one invited. Sign out and use the invited address, or ask your admin to re-invite the address you use.
- **Invitation link expired** — invitations last **7 days**. Ask your admin to send a fresh one.
- **In more than one company?** — use the company switcher in the sidebar. See [Switching companies](/settings/switching-companies).
