Joined a company on CTRL? Here's the whole first week.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept your invitation and sign in">
    One click from the email — see [Accepting an invitation](/getting-started/accepting-an-invitation). You'll land on your **Personal Dashboard**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check your profile">
    Open **Settings → Profile**: confirm your name, add a photo, and — most usefully — fill in your **Role summary** or upload a CV. This powers sharper AI OKR suggestions. See [Your role profile](/settings/role-profile).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your check-in slot">
    In **Settings → Notifications**, keep the company default or choose your own check-in day and time. See [Check-in schedule](/check-ins/schedule).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at your OKRs">
    Your dashboard lists **My OKRs** for the current cycle. If it says _"Your manager will set your OKRs for this cycle"_, that's normal — nudge your manager if nothing appears. If you have no manager, you can add your own with **Add OKR**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the Company page">
    The **Company** entry in the sidebar shows the company objectives and everyone in the company — expand a person to see their OKRs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your first check-in">
    When the **"This week"** banner shows your due date, click **Submit weekly check-in** and walk through the four steps. See [Submitting a check-in](/check-ins/submitting).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  That's the whole rhythm: keep your OKRs honest, check in weekly, and use blockers to ask for help early.
</Tip>
