The weekly check-in is the heartbeat of CTRL. Once a week — by default on a day and time your company chooses — you spend a couple of minutes recording how your work is going. That single update keeps your OKRs current, tells your manager where you're blocked, and feeds the company-wide picture.

## The four steps

Every check-in walks through the same four steps:

1. **Progress** — give each of your key results a fresh measure and status. See [Updating key results](/check-ins/updating-key-results).
2. **Blockers** — describe your biggest blocker, even if it seems small. See [Reporting blockers](/check-ins/reporting-blockers).
3. **Mood** — a one-tap pulse on how your week felt. See [Recording your mood](/check-ins/mood).
4. **Notes** — an optional open box for wins and observations. See [Adding notes](/check-ins/notes).

## Where check-ins show up

- Your **Personal Dashboard** shows a "This week" banner with a **Submit weekly check-in** button until you've submitted. Afterwards it changes to "You are all caught up."
- Your **manager** sees your latest mood, blocker, and key-result updates on their Team Dashboard.
- **Company owners** see roll-ups — check-in completion, open blockers, and AI-generated themes — on the Company Dashboard.

<Note>
  Check-ins are transparent by design: your manager and company owners can read what you submit. They are a pulse check, not a performance review.
</Note>
