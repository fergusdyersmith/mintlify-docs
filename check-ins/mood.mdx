The third step of the check-in — **How are you feeling?** — is a single tap. Pick the option that best matches your week:

| Mood | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Great** | Energised |
| **Good** | Steady |
| **Neutral** | Coasting |
| **Struggling** | Need support |

## Who sees your mood

- Your **manager** sees your latest mood next to your name on the Team Dashboard, and a **Mood this week** sentiment mix for the whole team.
- **Company owners** see an aggregated **How the business is feeling** mix on the Company Dashboard — individual moods roll up into the bigger picture.
- Your mood also appears on your past check-ins, visible to you, your manager, and owners.

<Note>
  Picking **Struggling** is a feature, not a failure — it's the clearest signal you can send that you need support, and it's designed to start a conversation.
</Note>
