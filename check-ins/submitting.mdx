You can start your check-in from two places — both open the same four-step flow:

- **Personal Dashboard** → the "This week" banner → **Submit weekly check-in** (opens as a modal).
- The **Complete your check-in** link in your reminder email (opens the full-page wizard).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Progress — Key results">
    Give each key result a one-line update and a fresh measure: a new value (a percentage, a 1–10 score, or a status, depending on how the key result was set up), a status — **On track**, **At risk**, **Off track**, or **Done** — and optional notes. See [Updating key results](/check-ins/updating-key-results).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Blockers — Biggest blocker">
    Describe anything slowing you down, even if it seems small. Your manager sees this on their Team Dashboard. See [Reporting blockers](/check-ins/reporting-blockers).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pulse — How are you feeling?">
    Pick one of four moods: **Great**, **Good**, **Neutral**, or **Struggling**. See [Recording your mood](/check-ins/mood).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open box — Anything else?">
    Optional space for wins, observations, or anything that doesn't fit elsewhere. See [Adding notes](/check-ins/notes).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What happens when you submit

- The values and statuses you entered are written straight onto your live key results — no separate update needed.
- Your dashboard banner switches to **"You are all caught up."**
- Your manager and company owners can see the check-in immediately.

<Tip>
  Honest beats polished. A candid blocker or a "Struggling" mood is exactly the signal that gets you support — bullet-point brevity is fine.
</Tip>
