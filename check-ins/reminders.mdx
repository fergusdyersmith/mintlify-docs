CTRL sends two kinds of nudges about your weekly check-in. Both can be switched on or off individually in **Settings → Notifications**.

## Due-today reminder

On the morning your check-in is due (sent around 09:00), you'll get:

- An **email** — by default titled _"Your weekly check-in is due today"_ — with a **Complete your check-in** button that opens the check-in wizard directly.
- A matching **in-app notification** in the bell menu.

You'll only get this if you haven't already submitted, and at most once per check-in. Admins can customise the email's subject and body — see [Email templates](/admin/email-templates).

## Overdue notification

If your due time passes and you still haven't submitted, CTRL raises an **in-app notification**: _"Your weekly check-in is overdue."_ This one doesn't email you — it only appears in the bell menu and on your Notifications page.

## Turning reminders on or off

In **Settings → Notifications** you'll find two toggles:

- **Weekly check-in reminder** — the due-today email and notification.
- **Overdue check-in reminder** — the overdue in-app notification.

<Note>
  Reminders follow your personal check-in day and time if you've set one — see [Check-in schedule](/check-ins/schedule).
</Note>
