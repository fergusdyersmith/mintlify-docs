The first step of every check-in lists each of your key results for the current cycle and asks for a one-line update and a fresh measure.

## For each key result you set

- **A new value** — the format depends on the key result's type:
  - **Percent** — enter the current percentage (e.g. 65).
  - **Scale 1–10** — enter the current score.
  - **Status** — the status itself is the measure.
- **A status** — **On track**, **At risk**, **Off track**, or **Done**. See [OKR statuses](/okrs/statuses).
- **Notes (optional)** — a line of context your manager sees alongside the number.

## Why it matters

Whatever you enter is written directly onto the live key result when you submit — the check-in _is_ how OKR progress gets updated in CTRL. Skipping check-ins means your OKRs go stale and your objective's health can't be trusted.

<Note>
  Progress is calculated from these values: percent and scale key results score current ÷ target, and status key results score 100% when **Done**. Details in [How progress is calculated](/okrs/progress-calculation).
</Note>
