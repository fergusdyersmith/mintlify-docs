The second step of the check-in asks one question: **what's your biggest blocker this week?** Anything slowing you down counts — even if it seems small.

## How your blocker is used

- CTRL asks its AI to compress what you wrote into a short headline (four words or fewer), so your manager can scan blockers across the whole team at a glance.
- On the **Team Dashboard**, your row shows a **Blocked** tag; hovering it reveals your full blocker text.
- Blockers are counted in the **Open blockers** stat on the Team and Company Dashboards, and recurring blockers across the company are grouped into **Blocker themes** on the Company Dashboard.

## Writing a useful blocker

- Name the thing you're waiting on, not just the feeling: "Waiting on legal sign-off for the contract" beats "stuck".
- One blocker is enough — the step asks for your _biggest_.
- If nothing is blocking you, say so or leave it empty; your row shows **No blockers**.

<Tip>
  Blockers are the single fastest way to get help in CTRL — they're the first thing managers look at each week.
</Tip>
