Every week of the current OKR cycle has one check-in per person. Your due date is set by:

1. **Your personal check-in day and time**, if you've set one — in **Settings → Notifications**.
2. Otherwise, the **company default**, set by your admin in **Admin → OKR Cycle** (out of the box this is **Friday at 17:00**).

## What you'll see

- The **"This week"** banner on your Personal Dashboard shows the due day and a countdown tag: _Due today_, _Due tomorrow_, or _Due in N days_.
- The banner also shows **Week X of Y** — where you are in the current cycle.
- Once you submit, the banner collapses to **"You are all caught up"** until the next week starts.

## Changing your check-in slot

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Notifications">
    From your avatar menu, choose **Settings**, then **Notifications**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your day and time">
    Under **Your check-in day** and **Your check-in time**, pick a slot that suits your week — or tick **Use company default** to follow the company-wide setting.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save preferences">
    Your reminder emails and due dates now follow your personal slot.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Admins set the company-wide default day and time — see [Cycle settings](/admin/cycle-settings).
</Note>
