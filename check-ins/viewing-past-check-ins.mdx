Every submitted check-in has its own read-only detail page showing exactly what was recorded that week:

- **Blocker** — the blocker reported, if any.
- **Mood this week** — the pulse selected.
- **Progress** and a **Key results updated** table — each key result with its update, status, and notes.
- **"In their words"** — the open-box notes.

## Where to find them

- **Your own** — open any check-in from your Personal Dashboard or from links in notifications.
- **A team member's** (managers) — open their profile from the Team Dashboard; their **Recent check-ins** for the cycle are listed with mood and blocker, and each opens the full detail page.
- **Anyone's** (owners) — owners can open any check-in detail across the company.

## Who can see a check-in

A check-in is visible to its author, the author's manager, and company owners. Colleagues outside that chain can't read it.

<Tip>
  Before a 1:1, skim the last few weeks of check-ins — mood trend plus recurring blockers is usually the whole agenda.
</Tip>
