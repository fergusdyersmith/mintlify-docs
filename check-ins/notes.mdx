The final step of the check-in — **Anything else?** — is an optional open text box. Use it for anything that doesn't fit the other steps: wins worth celebrating, observations about how the team is working, ideas, or context for the numbers you just entered.

## Where your notes go

- They appear in the **"In their words"** section of your check-in detail page, visible to you, your manager, and company owners.
- Across the whole company, notes are grouped by AI into **Feedback themes** on the Company Dashboard — "what people are talking about" — so patterns surface without anyone reading every check-in individually.

## What to write

- A win: "Closed the Acme renewal two weeks early."
- An observation: "Standups are drifting past 30 minutes."
- Context: "KR2 dipped because we paused for the security audit."

Or nothing at all — the step is genuinely optional, and skipping it doesn't hold up your check-in.

<Tip>
  If you want leadership to notice something, write it here. Notes are the raw material for the company-wide themes owners review each week.
</Tip>
